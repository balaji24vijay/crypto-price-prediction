POWER BI interview questions
=============================
1.Why do we use a primary key in a database or data model?
"A primary key is a column that uniquely identifies each record in a table. It must contain unique values and cannot contain duplicate or NULL values."

Which table should be placed at the centre of the Star Schema, and why?
"FactSales is placed at the centre of a Star Schema because it stores transactional data, including numerical measures such as Sales, Profit and Quantity. It connects to dimension tables such as Product, Region and Date through foreign keys."
